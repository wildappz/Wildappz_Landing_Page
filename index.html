Tapin

Meet the right people. At airports. Across the city.

Tapin isn't another app for scrolling through profiles. It's built for the moment you're already somewhere with time to spare — an airport lounge, a co-working space, a café — and shows you verified, high-intent people nearby: founders, investors, recruiters, travellers.

It's rolling out first across Hyderabad's busiest professional zones — HITEC City, Gachibowli, Financial District, Madhapur, T-Hub — plus the airport, with cafés, malls and open spots joining the map as auto-detected zones.

  • Founders, investors & recruiters
  • Freelancers & corporate professionals
  • Business travellers & event-goers

Leosden

Activewear built for the workout — and the rest of the day.

LeosdenWhere fitness meet style

Leosden makes fitness clothing people actually want to wear past the gym door — comfortable, well-fitted, and made for movement without looking like activewear.

It's early days by design. Rather than launching a huge catalogue, the focus is on a small range done well: real customers, better fit, better fabric, and a community that keeps coming back.

  • Gym-goers, runners & fitness regulars
  • Athleisure-minded young adults
  • Anyone who wants one wardrobe for training and daily life
